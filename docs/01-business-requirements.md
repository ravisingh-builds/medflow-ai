# Business Requirements Specification (BRS)

## Project Information

**Project Name:** MedFlow AI

**Client:** Rysk Labs (Simulated)

**Version:** 0.1

**Prepared By:** Lead AI Engineer

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Executive Summary

MedFlow AI is an AI-powered Patient Acquisition & Revenue Recovery Platform designed to automate the process of qualifying leads, scheduling appointments, and assisting clinic staff through intelligent workflows.

The objective is to reduce manual work, increase appointment conversion rates, and improve the overall patient experience.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Healthcare clinics often receive leads through online advertisements, websites, and referral channels.

Receptionists manually contact patients to gather information and schedule appointments.

This manual process introduces several problems:

- Delayed response to new leads.
- Missed opportunities due to unanswered calls.
- Repetitive administrative work.
- Inconsistent follow-up process.
- Lack of visibility into conversion performance.

The clinic requires an AI-powered workflow capable of automating repetitive tasks while keeping staff in control of important business decisions.

---

# 3. Business Objectives

The platform should:

- Respond to new leads quickly.
- Automatically qualify patients.
- Book appointments with minimal human intervention.
- Reduce receptionist workload.
- Increase appointment conversion rate.
- Provide visibility into business performance.

---

# 4. Stakeholders

## Primary Stakeholders

- Clinic Owner
- Receptionist
- Patient

## Secondary Stakeholders

- Marketing Team
- Clinic Administrator
- AI System Administrator

---

# 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)

The project will be considered successful if it can improve the following business metrics:

- Lead response time
- Appointment booking rate
- Receptionist workload
- Appointment completion rate
- Missed lead percentage
- Overall operational efficiency

---

# 6. Assumptions

- Clinics already have internet access.
- Staff members are comfortable using a web dashboard.
- Patients provide accurate contact information.
- Google Calendar will be used for appointment scheduling in Version 1.

---

# 7. Constraints

- Project duration is four weeks.
- Version 1 focuses on a single clinic.
- Voice calling is excluded from Version 1.
- SMS integration is excluded from Version 1.
- HIPAA compliance is outside the scope of this learning project.

---

# 8. Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded:

- Payment processing
- Insurance verification
- Multi-clinic management
- AI voice calling
- Facebook Ads integration
- Revenue forecasting

---

# 9. Deliverables

The project will include:

- Production-ready backend
- Modern web dashboard
- AI qualification workflow
- Appointment scheduling workflow
- Documentation
- Docker deployment
- Architecture diagrams
- API documentation
